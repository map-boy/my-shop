# Karibu Fit: trust tasks

Goal: until people know Karibu Fit, the job is not "sell more". It is "be easy to trust".
The admin opens on the **Tasks** page, which shows the daily routine below.

## Every day
- Post on WhatsApp Status: one product, a real photo or video, a Kinyarwanda caption, the link
- Answer every message within 5 minutes
- Ask one customer for a photo wearing the clothes
- Follow up with one creator or campus leader
- Post one try-on video (TikTok or Instagram)
- Check photos and stock of the top items

## Week 1: remove the risk
- [ ] Pay on delivery for Kigali, shown clearly on every product page
- [ ] "Don't like it, don't pay" guarantee and a 3-day exchange, written on the site
- [ ] Founder video in Kinyarwanda: who you are, where you work, what Karibu Fit is
- [ ] Pre-filled WhatsApp message on every product
- [ ] WhatsApp Business profile: logo, description, catalog
- [ ] Real address or pickup point, phone, Instagram and TikTok visible on the site

## Month 1: prove you are real
- [ ] Collect 10 to 20 real customer photos or reviews (discount in return)
- [ ] Gift clothes to 5 micro-influencers or campus leaders
- [ ] One pop-up stand at a university or market
- [ ] A group-order offer for one class, church youth group or sports team
- [ ] Weekly try-on live session with Q&A in Kinyarwanda

## Later: once people know the brand
- [ ] Agent program: recruit trusted people in each community
- [ ] Repeat-customer offers and referral rewards
- [ ] Push notifications
- [ ] Personalization and recommendations (only worth it with repeat customers)

## How to read the results
- Few visitors: a traffic problem. Post and partner more.
- Visitors but no WhatsApp clicks: a trust or offer problem. Work on week 1 and month 1.
- Clicks but no sales: price, reply speed or delivery.
